# Greca House

Ask an AI assistant for a two-bedroom flat in Pangrati within your budget. With the tools in this organisation, it can search Greca House's current listings, open one, check our dated asking-price research for that neighbourhood and estimate the transfer tax, notary and Cadastre costs of the purchase. The tools return links to the listing or report on grecahouse.com.

Greca House is an independent brokerage. Iryna Rondyak, founder and owner, runs it from Panepistimiou 44 in Athens, and her work in Greek real estate goes back to 2007.

## The MCP server

```
https://grecahouse.com/mcp
```

- Streamable HTTP, no sign-in and no key
- Read-only: a call never creates an enquiry
- Five tools: listing search, listing details, budget comparison by area, buying-cost estimate, market research
- Official MCP Registry name: `com.grecahouse/greek-property` (1.1.1)

## Research

Our research takes dated snapshots of asking prices across Greca House listings and releases them under CC BY 4.0, with the method published alongside. The snapshot dated 1 October 2026 counts 663 of our listings for sale that showed an asking price. Its median for Athens-area apartments is 3,667 EUR per m2, from 197 listings. The figures cover Greca House listings and their asking prices only.
